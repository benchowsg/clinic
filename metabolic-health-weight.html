How fast will I see results on GLP-1 therapy?

Most patients notice reduced food cravings within the first few days. Significant weight loss typically begins within 2 to 4 weeks, with optimal fat loss building over 3 to 6 months.

Do I need to come back to the clinic every month?

Are there side effects with GLP-1 weight loss?

Mild nausea or temporary indigestion can occur during the first few days of starting or stepping up a dose. Our doctor uses precise titration schedules to minimize any discomfort.
