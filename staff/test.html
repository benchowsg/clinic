Off-Site Firebase Backend & Email Extension Setup

Connect your Firebase project (`asia-southeast1` Singapore) so staff records, uploaded MCs/SAF100s, and email triggers reside outside the website HTML.

Security & Email Architecture

  • No Sensitive Data in HTML: Staff directory, leave requests, and email logs sync directly to Cloud Firestore (`staff_profiles`, `leave_requests`, `mail`).
  • Private File Vault: MCs, death certificates, and SAF100s upload to private Firebase Cloud Storage (`attachments/{staffId}/...`) with short-lived signed download URLs.
  • Trigger Email Extension: Installing Firebase's firestore-send-email extension watches the mail collection and sends via your clinic's Google Workspace / SMTP secret.
  • Zero Contact Numbers / Salary: Neither phone numbers nor salary figures are stored or rendered anywhere in the application.
