Frequently Asked Questions

Why is a comprehensive female metabolic check important if my annual health screening was normal?

Basic health screens check for overt disease rather than functional wellness. They often overlook early metabolic slowing, tissue nutrient depletion, or perimenopausal hormone shifts that drive symptoms like persistent fatigue, brain fog, and stubborn weight gain.

How do hidden iron stores affect a woman's energy and metabolism?

Standard blood counts only check circulating red blood cells. However, internal iron storage tanks can become severely depleted long before anemia appears on standard tests, leading to severe fatigue, poor exercise recovery, and hair shedding in women.

What if my thyroid checks seem normal but I still feel sluggish and cold?

A complete metabolic evaluation assesses active tissue conversion and total energy output rather than relying on a single baseline signal, helping pinpoint whether sluggishness stems from thyroid activity or another metabolic axis.
