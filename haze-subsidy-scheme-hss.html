Haze Subsidy Scheme (HSS)

As an accredited Primary Health Partnership Clinic (PHPC), A Healing Heart Medical Clinic provides affordable, subsidised care for acute and chronic haze-related medical conditions.

What is covered in this flat fee?
The flat fee covers your doctor's consultation, basic investigations, and prescribed medications for covered haze-related conditions during this visit.

6 Eligible Haze Conditions

Subsidies apply when diagnosed with or treated for any of the following haze-aggravated conditions:

URTI
Cough, sore throat, cold, viral fever
Asthma
Acute asthma flare-ups or management
Bronchitis
Airway inflammation & persistent coughing
Conjunctivitis
Eye redness, itching, haze irritation
COPD
Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease
Allergic Rhinitis
Nasal allergies, sneezing, runny nose
What if I am treated for non-haze conditions during the same visit?

Treatments, medications, or blood tests for non-haze conditions (e.g., skin rashes, high blood pressure, diabetes) are billed separately under standard clinic charges. However, our clinic will not charge a second consultation fee for treating non-haze conditions during the same consultation. You may also use standard CHAS or MediSave subsidies where applicable.

Frequently Asked Questions

No registration or pre-application is required. Eligibility is verified instantly at our counter when you present your physical or digital NRIC or CHAS / PG / MG card via the Singpass / HealthHub app.

Seniors aged 60 and above can use Flexi-MediSave to cover their $5 or $10 co-payment. For chronic conditions (such as Asthma or COPD) managed under CDMP, standard MediSave500/700 withdrawal rules apply.

Under MOH guidelines, medications prescribed under the scheme are strictly limited to the necessary quantity required for the immediate episode of care. Prolonged advance stockpiles are not permitted under the bundled scheme fee.

Please bring a valid Singapore NRIC, Birth Certificate (for children $\le$ 18), or present your digital card on the Singpass / HealthHub app upon registration at our clinic counter.
