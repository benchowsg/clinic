Frequently Asked Questions

Why is a comprehensive metabolic check important if my routine annual checkup was clear?

Basic health screens often evaluate general health indicators without looking deeper into early metabolic resistance, tissue nutrient stores, or hormonal shifts that drive symptoms like fatigue or stubborn weight gain.

How does iron storage affect metabolic energy?

Standard blood counts only check circulating red blood cells. However, your internal iron storage tanks can become severely depleted long before anemia shows up, leading to severe fatigue, low energy, and hair thinning.

What if my thyroid checks seem normal but I still feel sluggish?

A complete evaluation looks beyond basic signals to assess active tissue conversion and total metabolic output, helping pinpoint whether energy sluggishness is rooted in thyroid performance or another system.
