Beyond the Scale: Executive Female Metabolic & Hormonal Evaluation

A woman’s metabolism is driven by a complex interplay of estrogen, insulin sensitivity, thyroid output, and iron stores. Stop guessing why you feel tired, stuck on a weight plateau, or foggy—get clear, actionable clinical data.

🌸 Executive Screening Highlights

  • Beyond Fasting Sugar: Includes Fasting Insulin & HOMA-IR to detect early insulin resistance years before blood sugar spikes.
  • Full Thyroid Output: Measures TSH, Free T4, and Free T3 to assess your metabolic engine.
  • Complete Hormonal Axis: Evaluates Estradiol, Progesterone, FSH, LH, Total Testosterone, Prolactin, and Morning Cortisol for perimenopause, PCOS, or stress shifts.
  • Cellular Energy Reserves: Comprehensive Serum Ferritin, Full Iron/TIBC, Vitamin D (25-OH), B12, and Folic Acid panel to catch hidden fatigue drivers.
  • Cardiometabolic Protection: Complete Lipid Panel, Liver (ALT/AST/GGT) and Kidney function, and hs-CRP vascular inflammation check.

Many women are told their standard blood tests are "completely normal," yet they still struggle with persistent fatigue, unexplained abdominal weight shifts, hair thinning, or brain fog. Standard screening packages often miss subtle hormonal transitions, silent insulin resistance, or depleted iron stores (low ferritin).
