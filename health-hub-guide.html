HealthHub & NEHR (Health Information Act, HIA) FAQ

Why can't I see my latest medical report or results on HealthHub yet?

Under the Ministry of Health's (MOH) progressive rollout of the Health Information Act (HIA), patient-view capabilities for private healthcare providers are being phased in by service category. While public healthcare institutions are already connected, private clinics and independent clinical laboratories are officially scheduled to connect and begin contributing data to the NEHR by 1 September 2027. Until then, our medical desk provides full physical or digital copies of your lab and diagnostic reports directly to you during your consultation.

What changes for my private clinic records starting September 2027?

By 1 September 2027, under Batch 1 of the Health Information Act (HIA), all licensed private clinics across Singapore, including A Healing Heart Medical Clinic, will systematically contribute key health information to the National Electronic Health Record (NEHR). This means that from September 2027 onwards, your private clinic consultation summaries, active prescriptions, coded diagnoses, lab test results, and vaccination records will become directly visible to you within your HealthHub app alongside your public hospital records.

What results from A Healing Heart Medical Clinic can I view on my HealthHub app?

Under the Health Information Act (HIA), patient access to records in HealthHub is expanding progressively. Currently, you can view your vaccination history, subsidy statuses, and selected public health records. As MOH completes the phased onboarding of private General Practitioner (GP) clinics and clinical laboratories into the HealthHub patient-view, your private lab test results, prescriptions, and clinical summaries will become directly accessible in the app. In the meantime, our medical desk provides full copies of all your reports directly to you.

Is my health data secure under the Health Information Act?

Yes. Your medical data is strictly protected under the statutory framework of the HIA. The Act mandates strict cybersecurity standards, encrypted data transmission, role-based access controls for treating clinicians, and comprehensive audit logs that track every single instance a record is accessed. Unauthorized access or data misuse carries severe regulatory penalties under MOH oversight.

Do I need to pay for NEHR integration or record uploads?

No. Mandatory contribution to the NEHR under the HIA is a statutory requirement aimed at building a unified national medical summary to improve patient safety, prevent adverse drug reactions, and reduce redundant diagnostic testing. There is zero charge to patients.

Can I opt out of contributing data to the NEHR?

Under the Health Information Act (HIA), all licensed healthcare providers in Singapore are legally mandated to contribute required clinical summaries to the NEHR to maintain a complete national health registry. While contribution cannot be opted out of, you hold full authority over your Access Preferences within the HealthHub app. You can choose to restrict which specific healthcare institutions can view your NEHR records. Please note that essential safety data, such as severe drug allergies and vaccination histories, remains accessible to authorized doctors to protect you in emergency situations.
